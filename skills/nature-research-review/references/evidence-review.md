# Evidence protocol

This protocol is an original review method, not a Nature requirement. It controls how this skill turns sources into advice.

## Evidence classes

| Class | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Current flagship Nature instructions | Journal/article/stage rules within stated scope | A rule for every Nature Portfolio journal |
| Current Nature Portfolio policy | Shared policy within its declared scope | Automatic resolution of contradictory flagship wording |
| Design-specific reporting statement | Required/recommended information within the statement's scope | Validity, causality, or quality merely from checklist completion |
| Primary research or methods paper | Findings and conventions supported by that work | A universal writing rule or proof that a draft is novel |
| Published Article exemplar | How that particular version is written/presented | A mandatory template; proof that all its choices are optimal |
| Community agent skill | Workflow ideas worth independently evaluating | Scientific or Nature policy authority |
| Our review heuristic | A transparent, useful question or procedure | An externally validated threshold or journal mandate |

## Live-source verification

Search results are discovery aids. Open the primary page or document and read the relevant passage with surrounding qualifiers. Record `source ID | URL/DOI | title | section/page | publisher/journal | article type/stage | accessed date | evidence class | finding supported | limitations`. Use the installed `sources.json` as an index; do not assume the saved paraphrase is sufficient for a live rule.

Preserve words such as *ideally*, *typically*, *normally*, *should*, *must*, and *unless*. Do not change a recommendation into a disqualifying condition. Distinguish initial submission, revision, acceptance, and production; editor-specific instructions can supersede general formatting advice but do not authorize misconduct.

If two official sources differ, compare scope, stage, explicit effective date, and specialization. An explicit newer announcement can supersede older procedural text. If no clear precedence exists, cite both and mark a conflict. A conservative drafting target may be offered as an optional practical choice, never reported as the uniquely correct requirement. Ask the editor only when resolution is necessary for an actual submission decision; do useful scientific review meanwhile.

If full text is inaccessible, say what was available. Do not reconstruct details from the title or abstract. Never cite a source you have not opened as though you verified its contents. Public access and permission to redistribute are separate; link rather than copying papers or policy manuals into review outputs.

## Citation audit

For each checked citation, use a row with manuscript location, attributed claim, reference identity, accessed version, supporting source location, support assessment, and action. Support assessments: `supports as written`, `supports only a narrower claim`, `contradicts`, `background only`, `unverified`.

Verify authors, title, venue, year, DOI/identifier against publisher or repository metadata. Then read the actual relevant argument, methods, result, or limitation. Check the publisher's update/correction/retraction notices and version relationships before recommending a pivotal citation; absence of a noticed warning is not a comprehensive clean-bill certification. Distinguish a preprint from its published version and a review from the original evidence. Citation existence, metadata correctness, and claim entailment are three different checks.

Prioritize central novelty/comparison claims, direct quotations, numerical attributions, methodology support, and consequential contrary evidence. In a requested full reference audit inspect every reference; otherwise describe the selected scope and count. Do not add padding citations or recommend citation of the reviewer without a substantive reason. Keep bibliography formatting separate from evidence adequacy.

## Coverage and uncertainty

Maintain separate coverage for text, visual panels, Methods, supplement, data/code, references, literature search, and live journal rules. Use `inspected`, `partially inspected`, `not supplied`, `inaccessible`, `not applicable (reason)`. When a panel is illegible, that is evidence about the supplied rendering, not automatically the original figure.

Keep factual checks separate from subjective assessment. For example, an absent axis unit is observable; the best plot choice depends on the intended inference. A hypothesis about pseudoreplication requires knowledge of the experimental unit; unclear legend wording alone warrants a question, not a misconduct allegation.

For external novelty research, retain search dates, public queries, databases/publishers searched, inclusion reasoning, and contradictory results. Qualify conclusions to the search performed. Do not use citation counts, institutional prestige, impact factors, journal labels, or fluent prose as substitutes for evaluating evidence.

## Maintaining this skill

Refresh changed sources before changing claims. Source-backed advice belongs with the appropriate journal or field reference; reusable review mechanics belong here or in the entrypoint. Add Science as a separate verified profile/skill only after its own research, preserving journal and article-type boundaries. Do not create a blended “top journal” rulebook from incompatible policies.

The repository's research manifests preserve the source audit. The installed `sources.json` contains the same evidence records. Automated link/schema checks only detect structural problems; independent reading and realistic behavioral tests are still needed to evaluate advice.
