# Nature Article: editorial, structural and citation audit

Scope: the flagship **Nature**, ordinary original-research **Articles**. Checked 2026-10-10. This is an independent review procedure, not Nature's instructions or an acceptance predictor. Source IDs resolve in `sources.json`. Reopen applicable live guidance before identifying a compliance failure. For empirical style calibration, use [field-and-prose.md](field-and-prose.md); for every display item, use [figures.md](figures.md).

## 1. Establish the applicable standard

Record the journal, article type, discipline, draft date, submission stage, available files and any editor's letter. A published Article is evidence of an editorial outcome, not a universal template. A historical Letter, Nature Communications Article, Nature Reviews article or journalist's Nature feature is not a substitute for this profile. Registered Reports need their own current instructions; do not silently apply this ordinary-Article workflow.

Use these labels in findings:

- **Journal requirement:** the live source uses mandatory wording and applies to this stage and research type.
- **Journal guidance:** preserve qualifiers such as typical, ideally, normally, encouraged and preferred.
- **Field convention:** supported by the inspected comparison corpus, with counterexamples recorded.
- **Review judgement:** a reasoned recommendation linked to a specific claim, passage or figure.
- **Unverified:** an access, expertise, data or policy conflict prevents a conclusion.

Do not turn a soft target into a rejection criterion. Do not invent a house rule when official pages disagree; record both passages and seek stage-specific clarification only if it affects the requested work.

## 2. Separate editorial fit from evidential validity

Nature's author criteria emphasize original research, exceptional scientific importance and interdisciplinary relevance. Editors decide whether work merits external review and judge broad readership; technical validity alone does not establish editorial fit. Dataset, technique and therapeutic papers have distinct routes to significance; mechanistic novelty is not an unconditional requirement for every therapeutic paper. [N02]

Its referee criteria separately concern technical soundness, support for conclusions, novelty, importance within the field and wider scientific interest. Preprints do not themselves remove novelty. Editorial decisions are arguments assessed in context, not reviewer votes or numerical rankings. [N03]

**Our operational audit:** construct a claim map before editing prose. For each central claim record: manuscript location; exact scope; supporting panels/data; analysis or proof; assumptions; nearest prior work; strongest alternative explanation; remaining uncertainty. Then state two independent conclusions:

1. **What is established?** Identify the narrowest defensible result and what could invalidate it.
2. **Why might it matter?** Identify which scientific understanding or capability changes, for whom, and why. Separate demonstrated utility from conjectured applications.

If significance depends on an untested implication, retain that distinction. Do not inflate the paper to satisfy perceived prestige. A needed control should resolve a named threat to a central inference; label attractive follow-up research separately.

## 3. Apply the right submission stage

Initial submission is format-flexible within reason; a combined Word/PDF manuscript is encouraged, and PDF lines should be numbered. LaTeX authors supply PDF before acceptance. Titles must fit 75 characters including spaces. Methods are typically within 3,000 words but can be longer when necessary. Extended Data may initially appear among regular displays or in SI. [N05]

|---|---:|---:|
| Typical 6-page Article | about 2,500 words | 4 |
| Typical 8-page Article | about 4,300 words | 5–6 |

These are coupled page-budget examples, not independent entitlements. [N05] The text count includes summary plus body; larger displays consume more space. The summary is fully referenced, ideally no more than 200 words. Subheadings may be up to 40 characters including spaces. [N01]

Production instructions apply after acceptance in principle when requested. Keep final text and artwork separate; text includes legends and main tables. The final order is title, authors/affiliations, summary, main text, main references, tables, figure legends, Methods (ending with availability statements), associated references, acknowledgements, funding, contributions, competing interests, additional information and Extended Data legends/table titles and footnotes. Supply the final accepted version; preserve an editable working master when preparing submission copies. [N06]

**Our stage rule:** review scientific completeness first; do not spend an early-draft review manufacturing print layout. At revision, build an issue-to-response matrix from the editor's actual letter. Nature asks for point-by-point replies to referee comments; do not answer only the convenient objections. [N02]

## 4. Audit each section by its scientific function

The following questions are our review method, not a mandatory IMRAD template. Inspect the manuscript's actual architecture and the current field corpus before proposing headings or section moves.

### Title

Extract the precise claim a reader would infer without seeing the paper. Does the study support that scope, system, mechanism or generality? Are important qualifiers lost? Offer a shorter alternative only if it preserves the scientific meaning. Apply the current title instructions separately from this semantic check. Do not replace accepted technical nomenclature merely to make a title sound more dramatic.

### Summary paragraph

Nature's annotated model moves from accessible background to the unresolved problem, the main result, its relation to previous understanding and wider context. Its sentence counts are an instructional model, and additional background up to a longer paragraph is conditional on editorial judgement, not a general entitlement. [N07]

Make a sentence-level map: **known / gap / result / interpretation / implication**. Verify the main result against the body and figures, including direction, scale and limitations. Check each background citation against the exact assertion it supports. Ask whether a scientist outside the specialty can identify the problem and advance without decoding undefined abbreviations. Do not demand the literal words “Here we show”; the guide allows an equivalent. [N07]

### Opening/background

Identify the nearest established result and the precise unanswered question. Does the cited literature support the asserted gap, or only a broader statement? Is a competing explanation omitted? Keep the background sufficient to understand the design and result. A gap must be demonstrated, not manufactured with unsupported “little is known” language. Do not prescribe a fixed paragraph count or insist on a heading called “Introduction”.

### Results or substantive argument

For each subsection, identify the question, reason for the approach, observation, inference and role in the central argument. These need not appear as formulaic sentences. Trace every quantitative assertion to a visible panel, table, dataset or calculation. Check denominators, units, conditions, direction of comparison and stated uncertainty. Distinguish observations from model-dependent interpretation, descriptive associations from causal identification, and exploratory from confirmatory claims. Preserve a valid mathematical/theoretical argument's own structure rather than forcing an experimental narrative onto it.

### Discussion/closing synthesis

Ask what the result changes relative to the nearest prior evidence, what assumptions the interpretation needs and where the conclusion stops. Does the discussion engage the strongest alternative interpretation? Are model predictions, proposed mechanisms and applications marked by their actual evidential status? Test whether deleting the final promotional sentence would remove any scientific content. Do not require a separate “Discussion” or “Conclusion” section solely because another journal uses one.

### Methods

Build a reconstruction checklist for the actual study, using the field-specific reporting sources. Mark each item **documented / missing / unclear / not applicable**. A reader should be able to connect each result to the appropriate specimen, observation, data transformation, algorithm, model or derivation. Distinguish insufficient reporting from a demonstrated design flaw. For cited protocols, inspect the cited version and identify manuscript-specific deviations. Do not invent a sample-size rationale, blinding procedure, approval, exclusion rule or analysis that the authors did not perform.

The current reporting-policy reference determines which summaries, data deposits, code provisions and domain standards apply; do not apply every biomedical item to every discipline. [R03, R04]

### Availability, end matter and supporting material

Verify that identifiers resolve to the relevant versions and that claimed access matches the repository or access conditions; a dead link is not evidence that data never existed. Check factual declarations with the authors rather than rewriting them as if verified. Use authorship, funding and competing-interest policies for their respective statements. [R03, R07, R08; N06]

Treat main paper, Methods, Extended Data and Supplementary Information as one evidential record. The composition guide distinguishes their publication and editing treatment: Methods and Extended Data accompany online full text; supplementary files are separate, and are not copyedited. For publication preparation, ordinary additional figures belong in Extended Data rather than SI. [N08] The initial-stage exception is stated above. Study-specific exceptions require their own source; never discard necessary supporting evidence merely to obey a generic packaging rule.

For structural work, Nature's forms page supplies dedicated cryo-EM, NMR and X-ray crystallography tables. Check applicability and the current forms. [N11] Do not ask authors to complete a retired “editorial policy checklist”: that particular form has been removed. This does not remove other reporting obligations. [N12]

## 5. Audit citations for support before house style

Nature requires proper attribution of earlier intellectual and technical contributions, including when paraphrased. Related overlapping work must be disclosed; citing unpublished data also requires appropriate credit and permission. [N10]

**Our citation procedure:**

1. Resolve title, author, year, DOI or other identifier from the original publisher/repository. Record inaccessible sources as unverified rather than inventing bibliographic data.
2. Read the relevant source passage, methods or result. Classify support as **direct / partial / contextual / contrary / unverifiable**. A true citation can still fail to support the sentence.
3. Inspect all central novelty, mechanism, benchmark and priority claims. Search for the closest competing work using several names for the concept, not only the authors' terminology.
4. Preserve useful contrary evidence. Distinguish foundational work, later confirmation and a review summarizing them. Recommend an additional citation only with the precise claim it supports; never recommend a citation for journal prestige or self-promotion.
5. Check version and status: preprint versus peer-reviewed paper, subsequent correction, expression of concern or retraction. Explain the relevance of a status change rather than silently removing a reference. Do not treat an inaccessible reference as false.
6. Trace renumbering through main text, legends, Methods and Extended Data after structural edits. Do not strip a user's reference-manager fields from their working master; prepare any required submission copy separately.

**House-style snapshot:** references follow first occurrence, using superscripts; each number identifies one work. Include article titles. List all authors up to five, otherwise first author plus *et al.* Methods/Extended Data numbering continues. About 50 main references is guidance, not a universal maximum. Recognized preprints are allowed; work in preparation is discussed in text. For full punctuation, book/web/dataset formats and exceptions, consult the live guide. [N01]

## 6. Produce an informative, bounded review

Nature's report guide asks for assessment of results, validity, originality/significance, methods/data, uncertainty, conclusions, improvements, literature and clarity; it expects supplementary evidence to be considered and unassessed areas disclosed. Scientific criticisms belong in the author-facing report, without contradictory private criticism. [N04]

**Our finding format:** location → observed issue → supporting evidence → effect on interpretation → minimum useful correction → uncertainty. Separate a fatal unsupported conclusion from a fixable reporting omission and from optional polish. If raw data or expertise are missing, say what could and could not be assessed. Do not claim a polished manuscript is scientifically valid because its writing resembles published Nature work.

For revision review, distinguish **addressed / partly addressed / unresolved / new issue**, quote only enough of each response to locate it, and check the revised manuscript itself. A response promising an edit is not the edit.

## 7. Known policy drift and source conflicts

- **Transparent review:** the 16 June 2025 editorial states that research papers from new submissions on/after that change publish reviewer reports and author responses. Older opt-in language remains on some author/referee process pages. Use the dated change for that question, and do not assume every previously published paper has a review file. The announcement's accessible lead paragraph was checked; the paywalled remainder was not used. [N09; contrast N02, N03]
- **Summary:** the linked annotated model is dated 2019. Use it as an explanation of rhetorical structure, subject to current instructions and the editor's guidance. [N07]
- **Figures/captions:** official pages disagree on some dimensions and caption-length thresholds. Use the [figures.md](figures.md) conflict table; report the discrepancy rather than silently selecting a universal rule.
- **AI and confidentiality:** consult the integrity reference and current policy before using manuscript content in any workflow. Review support does not transfer scientific responsibility to this agent. [R01, R02]
