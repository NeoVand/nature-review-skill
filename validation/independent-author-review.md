# Independent author-side review of the synthetic plant-physiology packet

Review date: 10 October 2026. Target: flagship **Nature**, research Article, initial submission. This report reviews the invented exercise as requested; none of its numbers is treated as an actual research result. The source manuscript and figure have not been edited.

The excerpts support a descriptive comparison of fluorescence signals at one time point. They do not establish elimination of drought damage, generality across plants, or agricultural resilience. The principal statistical defect is explicit: irrigation was assigned to plots, but the analysis treats leaves within each plant as independent treatment replicates. Correcting the prose alone would leave that defect unresolved.

The packet has useful strengths: it states the randomization unit, provides both plot and leaf counts, identifies the time point, and explicitly limits the experimental scope. Its final main-text sentence already moves toward an appropriately local interpretation. Preserve those disclosures, while making the interpretation more precise.

## Minimal proposed wording

These are recommendations only. They intentionally narrow scientific meaning and must be reviewed by the author; they do not manufacture an analysed result.

**Title:** “Leaf fluorescence under additional irrigation in greenhouse plants”

**Replace the promotional claim with:**

> At 24 hours, the reported mean fluorescence signal across sampled leaves was 108 under additional irrigation and 100 in controls in the tested greenhouse setting. This descriptive difference does not establish elimination of drought damage; its statistical uncertainty requires an analysis that accounts for leaves sampled within plants.

This preserves the supplied means, comparison and time, while replacing unsubstantiated proof, universal scope and agricultural impact. The original factual design sentences can remain. Avoid retaining both this sentence and the existing local-response sentence if they become repetitive.

**Caption for the existing, unchanged synthetic image:**

> **Figure 1 | Illustrative fluorescence comparison in greenhouse plants.** **a,** Synthetic display of the reported mean fluorescence signals in control and additional-irrigation conditions, measured 24 hours after treatment. Eight plots were randomly assigned, four per condition, with one plant per plot and ten leaves measured per plant (four plots/plants and 40 leaf measurements per condition). Error marks are illustrative and do not represent calculated uncertainty. The displayed P < 0.001 is not validated plot-level inference: the described test treated leaves from the same plant as independent. This image is an exercise illustration, not research data.

This caption describes the image that actually exists, including the unsupported annotation; it does not pretend that annotation has been removed. For a future research figure, remove the unsupported P annotation and illustrative errors and regenerate the plot from the real analysis. A publication caption cannot be completed without the actual endpoint definition, units, uncertainty and statistical outputs. Those are author queries, not fields to guess.

## Revision order and remaining questions

1. Narrow the title, main claim, caption title and image title to the measured comparison. Preserve the synthetic disclosure.
2. Recover plot-labelled measurements and review the analysis with the correct unit of randomization. Until then, leave treatment significance and uncertainty unresolved.
3. Define the fluorescence endpoint and units; report species/genotype, irrigation amount and schedule, control water status, any drought induction, growth conditions, instrument/protocol and leaf-selection procedure. Establish what a signal change means before describing improved physiology or damage recovery.
4. Supply or locate randomization details, plot arrangement and independence, exclusions/missingness, sample-size rationale, blinding, replication history, and source-data/analysis availability. Their absence here means **not reported/not supplied**, not necessarily not performed.
5. Regenerate a real-data display only when those inputs exist, then revise its uncertainty, statistical annotation, axis encoding and accessible palette.

The excerpts establish neither a demonstrated advance beyond prior work nor relevance beyond the tested comparison. That limits any assessment of fit for Nature’s broad readership; it is not an acceptance/rejection prediction. Nothing here establishes that irrigation has no effect, either. The review can diagnose the analysis and claim mismatch, but cannot determine corrected significance, practical importance, or the biological meaning of an unspecified signal.
