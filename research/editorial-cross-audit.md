## Actionable findings

### A1 — Record the unresolved resolution discrepancy (medium)

Location: section 5, Figure specifications, and section 7, conflict table.

F04's Image resolution section gives 300 dpi as its minimum, but its Exporting section gives 450 dpi. The reference preserves only the first figure as a requirement. Add this internal discrepancy and avoid presenting one value as settled production compliance. Do not repair it by upsampling; request current production guidance if consequential. [F04](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/)

### A2 — Do not imply a schematic exception to figure-specific AI restrictions (medium)

Location: section 4, Figure-guide requirements, and section 7, AI-related questions.

F08's restriction encompasses generative AI in figures, not merely data repair. State that scope explicitly; do not infer permission for generated diagrams from the general AI framework. Recheck and resolve applicable policy before offering such artwork. The existing text does not explicitly claim an exception, but its narrower operational prohibition leaves one possible. [F08](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/image-integrity/)

This is a source-specific boundary to preserve, not a claim that the auditor has resolved every generative-AI use case under [R01](https://www.nature.com/nature/editorial-policies/ai).

### A3 — Mark the Extended Data area/page distinction as inference (medium)

Location: section 5, Extended Data specifications, and section 7, Extended Data dimensions row.

F06 labels its sizing figure as a page dimension. The reference renames it an artwork area. That may explain F03's different value, but the explanation is not established by the wording. Keep the conflict explicit and identify any proposed reconciliation as interpretation. [F06](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/extended-data-formatting-guidelines/), [F03](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/final-submission)
