# Independent audit of the Nature Article profile

Audit date: 2026-10-10. Auditor: separate research agent responsible for rigor/voice, not the author of `references/nature-articles.md`.

Scope: consequential journal-rule assertions in the then-current profile, checked against live primary Nature pages and the linked PDFs. This is an audit record, not a new set of journal requirements. It does not certify the whole skill, establish future policy stability, or independently validate a manuscript.

## Finding requiring a scope clarification

**Stage specificity for additional figures.** The profile's statement that ordinary additional figures belong in Extended Data should explicitly refer to final/publication preparation. The initial-submission page's “Extended data” section permits initial placement in the manuscript body or Supplementary Information. The existing study-specific-exception sentence does not explain this stage-specific allowance. Add it locally to prevent unnecessary initial-stage reformatting. Evidence: [N05, Extended data](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/initial-submission), alongside [N06, Supplementary Information](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/final-submission).

Disposition: reported to the root and profile author. The profile author added the initial-stage allowance and explicitly scoped the later packaging statement to publication preparation; the auditor checked the revised text. Resolved without modifying the profile from this audit task.
